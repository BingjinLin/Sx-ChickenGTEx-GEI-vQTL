# Sx-ChickenGTEx-GEI-vQTL
Genotype-by-environment interactions (G X E) drive complex trait variation in agriculture andprecision medicine, yet identifying their underlying molecular mechanisms remains challenging due tothe difficulty of directly measuring high-dimensional macro-environmental exposures at scale. Here,we address this limitation by leveraging the ChickenSexGTEx resource, comprising 280 whole-genome sequences, 7,969 multi-tissue RNA-seq profiles, 93 single-cell datasets, 865 metabolites of999 birds, and 24 complex traits of 1500 birds, to systematically map variance quantitative trait loci(vQTL) and dynamic expression interactions across tissues, sexes and other metadata records. The genetic control of gene expression variability exhibits markedly higher tissue and sex specificity than conventional mean-expression regulation(eQTL). Furthermore, cell-interaction QTL (cieQTL) analyses demonstrate that bulk tissue vQTL signals reflect both cell-type composition shifts and cell-intrinsic G X E regulation. Integrating these vQTL with metabolome-wide and complex trait association studies establishes that expression variability acts as a vital intermediate molecular layer governing lipid metabolism and phenotypic variation. Together, this framework advances our understanding of context-dependent gene regulation and their implications in higher-order complex phenotypes
